My name is Moon and here are artists that inspires me.


Matcha

Image by Matcha. Source: @matchach on X

This is a piece by Matcha, an illustrator from China. I chose it because I like how clean their colors are and how detailed the image feels. It shows the kind of color and light I want in my own art.



Kyoki Studio

Image by Kyoki Studio. Source: @IamKyoki on X

This is a set of Vtuber models by Kyoki Studio. I chose it because their art style feels unique, and I've used their customizable models before. It shows the kind of character design I want to learn from.



Chisa / Little Mewn

Image by Chisa (Little Mewn). Source: @littlemewn on X

This is a VTuber asset by Chisa (Little Mewn). I chose it because her rigs move smoothly and the art feels soft and alive. It shows the kind of Live2D work I want to learn.
